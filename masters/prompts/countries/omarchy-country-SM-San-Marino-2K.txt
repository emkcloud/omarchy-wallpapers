A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of San Marino at golden-hour sunset, seen from a winding hillside road on the Apennine foothills of Monte Titano. Composition: in the lower foreground, occupying the bottom third of the frame, a wide asphalt road sweeps in from the bottom-left corner and curves in a long S-bend toward the middle-right distance, its dark grey surface wet-polished and mirroring warm orange light; a solid white edge line runs along the left shoulder and a bright golden-yellow centre line traces the curve, with short white dashes beside it. On the right shoulder, a low pale concrete kerb and a small white roadside marker post stand at the very right edge; beyond it a steep grassy embankment rises out of frame, dotted with dry golden grasses, low shrubs and a single slim cypress silhouette at the far right against the sky.

Left foreground and midground: a broad grassy slope of sun-bleached golden-green meadow with scattered grey limestone rocks and tufts of tall grass. Standing on this slope in the left-middle of the frame, roughly one third up from the bottom, is a single crested porcupine (Hystrix cristata) in profile, facing left, at realistic scale — about 70–80 cm long, knee-high, far smaller than the boulders around it and tiny compared with the buildings behind; its long black-and-white banded quills are backlit and glowing against the low sun, its dark rounded body and short legs in shadow. The animal must be modest in size, clearly an animal on a hillside, never as large as a house or vehicle.

Midground left of centre: the dramatic limestone cliff of Monte Titano rises sheer from the meadow, pale grey rock streaked with vertical fissures and clumps of green scrub and small trees clinging to ledges. Crowning the cliff is the historic hilltop citadel of San Marino: on the left a tall square medieval stone watchtower (the Guaita / First Tower) with crenellated battlements, narrow arched windows and a slender pointed slate-grey spire, roughly 20–25 metres tall and clearly small relative to the whole cliff; a long crenellated curtain wall runs from it to the right along the ridge; behind and above the wall a compact cluster of terracotta-roofed stone houses steps up the slope; and at the ridge's highest point, right of centre of the citadel, a second larger fortress tower (the Cesta / Second Tower) with a square keep, a taller narrow bell-tower and battlemented walls, small green pines growing on the summit. Further right, on a separate lower wooded spur about a third of the way to the horizon, a third small isolated stone tower (the Montale) stands alone above the trees, noticeably smaller with distance.

Background: rolling Romagna–Marche countryside receding into layered blue-violet haze — soft green and gold hills, hedgerows, tiny distant farmhouses and copses, and ridge after ridge of low Apennine mountains fading to pale lavender at the horizon. The sun sits low just above the horizon slightly right of centre, a white-gold glow bleeding into peach and amber sky.

Sky occupies the upper half: gradient from warm gold and apricot near the horizon to soft cerulean and dusty blue at the top, filled with elegant wind-swept cirrus and lenticular cloud streaks tinted salmon-pink, rose and lilac, curling in long sweeping arcs across the upper left and upper right.

Lighting and atmosphere: warm low-angle backlight and rim light on the porcupine's quills, the grasses, the cliff edge and the tower stonework; long soft shadows across the meadow; gentle aerial perspective and light haze in the valleys; calm, serene, majestic mood.
