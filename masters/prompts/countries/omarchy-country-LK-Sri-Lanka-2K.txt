A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of the Sri Lankan hill country at golden-hour sunrise, an emerald landscape of terraced tea plantations, misty mountains, an ancient rock fortress and a white Buddhist stupa, with a winding tarmac road threading through the valley.

Foreground, left third: a grassy hillside slope covered in tall lush green grass, ferns and low tea bushes, sloping down from the lower-left corner toward the center. Standing on this slope, facing right in profile, is a single adult Sri Lankan elephant (Elephas maximus maximus): grey-brown wrinkled skin, small rounded ears, domed head, short pale ivory tusks, trunk hanging down and slightly curled. The elephant is realistically scaled — roughly 3 m at the shoulder, clearly smaller than the nearby trees, occupying about one sixth of the frame height, dwarfed by the mountains behind it; it must never look larger than a building. A few slender tropical trees and a leaning eucalyptus rise from the far-left edge, taller than the elephant. Dark soil, exposed rocks and grass blades catch warm rim light in the lower-left and bottom edge.

Midground, center and center-right: rolling hills entirely carpeted in neat contour rows of manicured tea plantation, the striped terraces curving with the topography in deep saturated greens. A narrow two-lane asphalt road enters from the center-right distance, snakes down in a long S-curve and hairpin through the tea fields, passing across the lower center of the frame, its pale grey surface and faint markings catching the light. At the lower center-right the road/rail line crosses a colonial-era stone viaduct with tall semicircular arches (Nine Arch Bridge style), half hidden by palms and dense green foliage. Scattered coconut palms, small clusters of jungle trees, and a couple of tiny distant tea pickers in colorful saris add human scale and confirm the vastness of the valley.

Right midground, on a green ridge about a third of the way in: a small white Buddhist dagoba (stupa) with a smooth bell-shaped dome, square harmika and a slender golden spire, beside a small temple pavilion with a red-tiled tiered roof and white walls. The stupa is modest in size — comparable to a two-storey building, tiny against the mountains — surrounded by palms and tea terraces.

Background, upper left: the great monolithic rock fortress of Sigiriya (Lion Rock), a colossal ochre-and-grey granite plateau rising abruptly some 200 m out of the jungle, its sheer striated cliff faces streaked with rust and lichen, flat summit crowned with ancient weathered brick ruins, terraces and a few wind-bent trees, a narrow stairway clinging to its right flank. Jungle-covered lower slopes surround its base. It dominates the left background but sits far away, hazy with atmospheric perspective.

Background, center and right: layered ranges of blue-green mountains and conical peaks receding into pale mist, valleys filled with low-lying fog catching the sunrise. On the far right, a large mountain silhouette with the sun rising just behind and to its left, low on the horizon, glowing warm orange-gold.

Lighting: early-morning golden hour, low warm sun on the right producing long soft shadows to the left, warm rim light on the elephant's back and on the grass, cool blue shadows in the valleys. Sky: deep blue at the top-left with soft cumulus clouds, transitioning to peach, apricot, salmon and pale gold near the horizon on the right; wispy cirrus catching pink light.

Color palette: emerald and lime greens, teal-blue mountains, warm ochre rock, cream white and gold on the stupa, peach and amber sky. Atmosphere: serene, tropical, humid, epic and tranquil.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, ultra wide establishing shot, 16:9 aspect ratio, highly detailed, sharp foreground detail with soft atmospheric depth, volumetric light and haze, rich natural color grading, National Geographic travel poster feel, 8K.
