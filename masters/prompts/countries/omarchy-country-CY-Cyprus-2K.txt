A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a golden-hour coastal landscape in Cyprus, seen from a sunlit hillside above the Mediterranean Sea, with a winding asphalt road descending from the lower center toward a small stone village by the shore.

Foreground: in the lower-left third, a dry grassy slope of pale golden grass, limestone boulders, thistles and low scrub, tilting down toward the road. Standing on this slope, in left-of-center foreground and facing right in profile, a single adult Cyprus mouflon ram (Ovis gmelini ophion) — realistic sheep size, roughly 90 cm at the shoulder, its body no taller than the surrounding shrubs and clearly much smaller than the buildings behind it — with a chestnut-brown coat, dark chest and neck patch, creamy muzzle and belly, slender legs, and thick curved horns spiralling backward and down beside its cheeks. Warm rim light catches its back and horns; a soft cast shadow falls to the right across the grass. To the far left edge, an old olive tree of moderate height (about 5 m), gnarled twin trunk, silvery-green foliage, its canopy occupying the upper-left corner and framing the scene. A wooden roadside guard post with reflectors sits at the lower-left where the road curves.

Midground center: a two-lane country road with a faded white edge line snakes in a smooth S-curve from the bottom-center of the frame up and to the left, then right, shrinking with distance as it passes the village and disappears behind low hills. Along its verges, dry grass, small olive and carob shrubs, and low dry-stone retaining walls.

Midground right: a small traditional Cypriot Orthodox chapel occupying roughly the right third of the frame, close to the viewer and larger than the mouflon by several times — a compact stone church of honey-coloured ashlar blocks with a broad, steeply pitched dark slate/timber protective roof (typical Troodos painted-church style), a shallow red terracotta-tiled dome on a drum with small arched windows on the right side, and a square stone bell tower with an arched opening and hanging bronze bell on the left of the chapel, both crowned with simple iron crosses. On the chapel's front gable, a small Byzantine fresco icon of a saint with a gold halo above a low arched wooden door. A dry-stone wall and rough stone terrace run in front of it along the road, with two small olive trees at its left corner. Behind and beside the chapel, a row of five tall dark-green cypress trees, considerably taller than the chapel, rising vertically against the sky on the right side.

Midground left-center: a hillside village of small vernacular houses with pale limestone and ochre plaster walls and red-tiled roofs, clustered in tiers down toward the beach, with slender cypresses among them and terraced vineyards and olive groves on the green-gold slopes to their right.

Background: a calm turquoise-to-steel-blue Mediterranean bay stretching across the center of the frame, a pale curving sand beach, and two large sea stacks rising from shallow water just offshore left of center — a big rounded limestone rock and a smaller companion, evoking Aphrodite's Rock (Petra tou Romiou). Beyond them, layered blue-grey coastal mountains and headlands recede to the horizon, with soft hills also on the far right.

Lighting and atmosphere: late afternoon into sunset, the sun a small brilliant white-gold disc low over the sea slightly left of center, casting a shimmering golden path on the water and warm amber light across the grass, stone walls and animal fur. Sky: gradient from warm peach and cream near the horizon to clear cerulean blue at the top, filled with wispy, swirling cirrus clouds catching pink and gold. Gentle haze in the distance, crisp detail in the foreground.

Color palette: warm gold, honey and ochre; terracotta red; olive and cypress green; turquoise and deep blue sea; soft peach and pale blue sky.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, matte-painting quality, 16:9 aspect ratio, wide-angle vista, highly detailed, natural realistic proportions, rich warm color grading, serene Mediterranean mood.
