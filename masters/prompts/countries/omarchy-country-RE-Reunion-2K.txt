A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of Réunion Island in the Indian Ocean at golden hour, seen from a high tropical ridge looking down over a Creole mountain village, a hairpin coastal road, an active volcano and a turquoise lagoon coastline. Elevated wide-angle vista, deep aerial perspective from lush green foreground to hazy sunlit ocean horizon.

BACKGROUND (upper left to center): a colossal eroded volcanic massif in the style of the cirques of Mafate and Salazie — jagged serrated basalt ridges, knife-edge aretes and needle-like pitons rising roughly 2,000-3,000 m, their vertical cliff walls streaked with thin silver waterfalls dropping hundreds of metres into a shadowed green amphitheatre. The massif occupies the left half of the frame, its summit crest reaching about two-thirds of the image height. A slender isolated rock spire (piton) stands in the mid-left-of-centre, dwarfed by the main wall behind it. Mid-right background: a broad low-profile shield volcano cone (Piton de la Fournaise) with dark bare lava flanks and a thin white plume of steam/gas drifting from its crater, small in the distance, its scale clearly smaller than the near massif because of the distance. To its right the coastline recedes: dark hardened lava fields meeting the sea, a pale golden crescent beach, turquoise-to-deep-blue reef water, gentle white surf lines, and the sun sitting just above the ocean horizon on the far right, casting a long shimmering gold path on the water.

MIDGROUND (centre): a small Creole hillside village perched on a green ridge, roughly 20-30 modest single-storey houses with brightly coloured walls (white, mint green, coral red, pale blue) and corrugated red and green roofs, each house realistically tiny compared with the mountains behind — a few pixels tall relative to the cliff wall. At the village centre stands a small white colonial Creole church with a square bell tower, a red-orange tiled roof and a modest steeple; the church is only slightly larger than the surrounding houses, human-scaled, about the height of three or four house storeys. A narrow two-lane tarmac mountain road winds up from the lower centre-right of the frame, passes through a short rock tunnel cut into a basalt spur at centre-right, curves in tight S-bends and hairpins past the village, and disappears over the ridge; the road is realistically narrow, its width equal to two small cars, with faded white edge markings and low guardrails. Farther right, thin ribbons of the same coastal road trace the green cliffs down toward the shore. Two or three white tropical seabirds (white-tailed tropicbirds) glide small in mid-air above the valley on the right, tiny against the landscape.

FOREGROUND (lower left): a weathered grey fallen tree branch angling in from the left edge, and resting along it a single Réunion ornate day gecko (Phelsuma), realistically sized at about 12-15 cm long — a small lizard, no larger than a hand, clearly not larger than the leaves and branch around it. Its body is vivid emerald and turquoise green flecked with tiny red-orange dots, with a bright red-orange eye ring, gripping the bark in profile, facing right, sharply in focus. Around and below it, dense tropical vegetation fills the bottom edge: broad banana and heliconia leaves, bromeliads, tree ferns, endemic screwpine (pandanus/vacoa) fronds on the lower right, tall slender palms on the left edge and lower right, and a few scarlet tropical flowers in the lower right corner.

LIGHTING AND ATMOSPHERE: late-afternoon golden-hour sunlight raking in from the right, warm amber rim-light on the ridges and grass slopes, cool blue-violet shadows filling the cirque, soft volumetric haze and light mist in the valleys, high wispy cirrus clouds catching pink and apricot tones against a clear cyan-blue upper sky that graduates to warm cream and gold near the horizon.

COLOR PALETTE: emerald and jade greens, basalt grey-brown, warm amber and honey highlights, turquoise and sapphire ocean, cream-gold sky, small accents of coral red and white from the rooftops.

STYLE: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, matte-painting quality, 16:9 aspect ratio, ultra sharp foreground detail with atmospheric depth of field into the distance, highly detailed, natural realistic proportions and scale between animal, buildings, road and mountains, wallpaper composition.
