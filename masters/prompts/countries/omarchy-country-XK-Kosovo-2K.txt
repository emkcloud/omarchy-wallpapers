A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a golden-hour landscape in Kosovo, evoking the historic town of Prizren seen from the green pasture slopes of the Sharr (Šar) Mountains. Wide panoramic vista, elevated viewpoint looking down a broad valley toward a warm setting sun on the distant horizon.

Foreground, left third: a steep grassy hillside covered in sun-lit summer grass, wildflowers and low shrubs, with a few small deciduous trees and slim conifers along the ridge. Standing in profile on this slope, at roughly one-third height from the left edge and low in the frame, is a single Sharplaninac (Šarplaninac / Illyrian Shepherd Dog), a large thick-coated livestock guardian dog with a broad head, dark grey-brown mask, short erect-tipped ears and a pale sandy-grey coat. It faces right, looking out over the valley. Realistic scale: the dog is about knee-to-thigh height of a person, roughly 65-70 cm at the shoulder — clearly much smaller than the trees behind it and only a tiny silhouette compared with the town below; it must never appear as large as a building or vehicle. Behind and to the far left, a small scattered flock of six to eight white-and-brown sheep grazes on the same slope, each noticeably smaller than the dog and each about one third the height of the nearby bushes, reinforcing correct scale.

Midground, center and center-right: a two-lane asphalt mountain road winds in a long S-curve from the lower-right foreground, sweeping left across the green hills and then curving back down toward the town. The road is narrow relative to the landscape, with faded white edge markings, grassy verges and low bushes along its shoulders, catching a soft warm sheen from the low sun.

Midground right: an Ottoman-era Balkan town in the valley — dense clusters of small white and cream houses with steep red terracotta tile roofs, packed along a shallow river. In the center-right of the town stands a classic Ottoman mosque with a lead-grey dome, arcaded porch and a single slender white minaret with a conical cap; two more thin minarets rise from the town further to the left, small and delicate against the haze. A short arched stone Ottoman bridge crosses the river at the lower right of the town, with cobbled approaches. All buildings are small in the frame — a house roof no wider than a few brush strokes — establishing the great distance and scale of the valley.

Right side, on a green rocky hilltop directly above the town: a large medieval stone hilltop fortress (a Kaljaja-style citadel) with long crenellated curtain walls, half-ruined square towers, grassy inner courtyards and crumbling ramparts following the ridgeline. The fortress is imposing but proportionate — its walls read as roughly three to four times the height of the houses below, and the whole complex occupies a modest portion of the right quarter of the image, not dominating the sky.

Background: receding layers of blue-grey mountain ridges fading into atmospheric haze, with a higher snow-streaked alpine range on the far left, forested foothills in the middle distance, and a soft luminous valley opening to the horizon at center-right where the sun is setting.

Lighting and atmosphere: late golden hour, warm low sun near the center-right horizon casting long soft shadows down the slopes and rim-lighting the dog, the grass and the fortress walls. Sky occupies the upper half: pale blue at the top with dramatic wind-swept cirrus and soft cumulus clouds lit peach, cream and apricot near the sun, cooling to lavender-blue at the edges. Color palette of emerald and olive greens, warm ochre and honey light, terracotta roof reds, cool slate-blue mountains and a glowing amber sky.
