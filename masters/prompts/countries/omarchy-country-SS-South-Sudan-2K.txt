A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a South Sudanese landscape at golden-hour sunset: the vast flat green floodplain savanna of the Sudd wetlands along the White Nile, with a winding tarmac road, a traditional Dinka/Nuer cattle camp of thatched tukul huts, long-horned Ankole cattle, and wading birds.

FOREGROUND (bottom of the frame, occupying the lower third): a smooth two-lane asphalt highway enters from the bottom-center-right and sweeps in a wide S-curve up and to the left, narrowing into the middle distance around a low grassy embankment. The road has a broken white center line and a solid yellow-ochre edge line on the right; its dark grey surface is polished by low sun so it mirrors warm amber and pale blue sky reflections. Along the bottom-left corner and up the left side rises a low earthen bank of red-brown laterite soil and tall dry golden grass tufts about knee-to-waist high, with sparse thin green shrubs; small stones and reddish gravel line the shoulder. On the right shoulder, low golden grass and a few pale rocks border the roadway.

LEFT MIDGROUND: on the grassy bank above the road, a small group of four slender antelope — impala/gazelle with lyre-shaped ridged horns on the standing male at the far left — stand and graze, correctly scaled: roughly 90 cm at the shoulder, clearly shorter than the road is wide and far smaller than any hut, their bodies only a small fraction of the frame height. Their reddish-tan coats and white bellies catch rim light from the low sun.

CENTER MIDGROUND: a broad, slow, silvery river meanders in wide S-bends from the center-left toward the horizon, its surface a mirror of pale gold and blue. Its banks are fringed with dense papyrus and tall reed beds, floating water-lily pads and shallow green marsh channels on the left, and pale sandbanks and low sandy islands in the center-right. Small isolated reed islets dot the water.

RIGHT MIDGROUND: a cattle camp on the flat, dusty right bank. Five to six circular tukul huts with conical thatched roofs and mud-and-wattle walls, each roughly 3–4 m tall and 4–5 m wide — human scale, clearly taller than the cattle and far larger than the antelope — cluster at the center-right and right edge; the largest sits at the right of center, a smaller one further left near the reeds, and one more at the far right partly cut by the frame. Thin wooden-stick fences and a low kraal enclosure sit between them, with a scattering of gourds and drying racks. Around and in front of the huts stands a herd of about twenty white and pale grey long-horned Ankole/Nilotic cattle with enormous sweeping crescent horns, roughly 1.3–1.5 m at the shoulder — half the height of the hut roofs. Several rest on the ground; two or three slim standing human figures, cattle herders in simple wraps, move among them at true human scale (about the same height as a cow's horn tips). Low white smoke from smouldering cattle-dung fires drifts in soft veils between the animals and huts, catching the sunlight. A single flat-topped acacia tree stands at the far right edge behind the huts, and a small leafy tree near the central hut.

BIRDS: a few tall wading birds — grey crowned cranes and long-legged storks — stand in the shallows and on the sandbank at center-right, about 1 m tall, and one large dark bird glides low above the water just right of center, near the horizon.

BACKGROUND: an immense flat green savanna plain stretches to a very low horizon line placed just below the upper third, speckled with distant dark green bush clumps and scattered trees, and a faint blue-grey line of very low hills on the far left. The sun sits just above the horizon at the center-right, a small brilliant white-gold disc laying a shimmering path down the river.

SKY (upper half of the frame): a wide luminous sunset sky graduating from warm amber and peach at the horizon to soft cyan-blue at the top, filled with layered cumulus and stratus clouds — larger billowing clouds in the upper-left and upper-right corners, thin streaks near the sun — their undersides lit orange-pink and their tops cool white-blue.

LIGHTING AND PALETTE: low warm golden-hour backlight, long soft shadows pulling toward the viewer, gentle atmospheric haze and volumetric light rays through dust and smoke; palette of amber, gold, peach, dusty rose, olive and emerald green, red-brown earth, silver-blue water and cool sky blue. Calm, expansive, serene pastoral atmosphere.

STYLE: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, wide-angle vista, deep perspective, 16:9 aspect ratio, highly detailed, crisp textures on grass, thatch, cattle hides and asphalt, soft depth-of-field falloff toward the horizon, natural realistic proportions between animals, people, huts and landscape.
