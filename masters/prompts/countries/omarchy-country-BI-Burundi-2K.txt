A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of Burundi, the "land of a thousand hills", at golden-hour sunset above the northern shore of Lake Tanganyika. The composition is built around a wide asphalt highway that enters at the bottom center-left of the frame, sweeps in a broad S-curve to the left foreground, then snakes back to the right and climbs over the green hills into the middle distance, shrinking to a thin ribbon before it disappears toward the lakeside town; the road has crisp white edge lines and a double yellow center line, its wet-looking dark surface catching warm orange reflections of the low sun, and it occupies roughly the lower third of the frame.

Foreground left: a small still pond or marshy water hole with reedy green banks and low leafy shrubs, tucked into the inside of the road curve. Half submerged in the pond, facing the viewer, is a single adult hippopotamus - only its broad head, shoulders and back above the waterline, realistically scaled at about 1.5 metres of visible width, far smaller than the nearby houses, its wet grey-pink hide catching the warm light. Standing on the grassy bank just to the right of the hippo are two grey crowned cranes (Balearica regulorum), Burundi's national bird: slender, roughly 1 metre tall, pale grey bodies, black-and-white cheek patches, red throat wattle and a spray of golden straw-like feathers on the crown; they are small accents, no taller than the roadside grass clumps beside them.

Foreground and midground right: a small rural Burundian hillside settlement lining the road. Three traditional round mud-walled huts with conical thatched grass roofs (about 3 metres tall each) sit just above the roadside among banana plants and broad-leaved trees. Further right, a modest rectangular whitewashed house with a corrugated red-orange metal roof and a woven reed fence stands at the very edge of the frame. In front of it, on the roadside verge, a small group of six to eight villagers in vivid red, white and yellow striped and wrapped traditional cloth walk in single file; several balance woven baskets and tall carved wooden drums on their heads, a nod to the Burundian royal drummers. The figures are human-scaled, reaching only about half the height of the house behind them.

Midground: rolling, densely cultivated hills covered in neat terraced tea and banana plantations, patchworked emerald and yellow-green, with scattered dark trees, red-earth footpaths and eucalyptus lines threading over the ridges. On the tall hill in the left third of the frame, silhouetted against the sky on the crest, stands a small pale stone pyramid monument on a stepped base - the Nile Source pyramid at Rutovu - modest in size, roughly 4 to 5 metres tall, reading as a bright white triangular marker on the green ridge rather than a large building. Below the hills on the right, a lakeside village of small red-roofed houses and palm trees clusters at the water's edge, with two long wooden fishing pirogues with triangular sails floating on the calm lake.

Background: the vast silver-blue expanse of Lake Tanganyika filling the center-right distance, its glassy surface split by a long shimmering golden sun-path. Beyond it, layered blue-violet mountain ranges of the Congo-Nile divide fade into haze, and the sun sits just above the far ridge line as a small brilliant white-gold disc.

Sky: two thirds of the frame, deep cerulean blue at the top with wispy, wind-stretched cirrus clouds catching pink, cream and amber light, softening into a broad glowing orange-gold band along the horizon.

Lighting and mood: warm low-angle golden-hour sunlight raking across the hills, long soft shadows, luminous atmospheric haze over the lake, gentle contrast between cool blue distance and warm green-gold foreground. Color palette: emerald and lime green, warm ochre and red earth, terracotta roofs, deep lake blue, amber and orange sky.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, ultra detailed, wide-angle landscape, 16:9 aspect ratio, sharp foreground detail with atmospheric depth, realistic proportions and scale, high dynamic range, desktop wallpaper quality.
