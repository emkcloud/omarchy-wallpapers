A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a tropical Panamanian landscape at golden-hour sunset, celebrating Panama's identity as the isthmus where dense rainforest meets the Panama Canal. Wide elevated viewpoint looking down a jungle-covered valley toward a broad, calm shipping waterway that snakes from the lower right of the frame into the hazy distance at the center horizon, its mirror-like surface reflecting orange and gold sky.

Foreground, lower left and lower center: a rust-red laterite dirt road, unpaved and slightly rutted, enters at the bottom center-left and curves in a long sweeping S-bend up and to the right, narrowing as it climbs a jungle ridge and disappearing behind vegetation in the midground; the road occupies roughly the lower third of the frame and is about three car-widths across at the bottom edge, narrowing to a thin ribbon as it recedes. Its shoulders are lined with broad-leaved tropical plants, heliconia, low ferns and glossy green shrubs, with warm sunlight raking across the wet clay surface.

Foreground left, standing on a grassy earthen bank just above the road: a single adult jaguar (Panthera onca) in profile facing right toward the water, roughly 1.7 m long body plus tail, shoulder height about 75 cm — realistically scaled, clearly smaller than the nearby shrubs and only a fraction of the height of the palms behind it, occupying about one tenth of the image width. Golden rosette-patterned coat rim-lit by the low sun, muscular build, tail curving down and back, calm alert posture.

Left edge and left midground: a wall of lowland rainforest — tall slender royal palms and cecropia trees rising 20–30 m, layered canopies of dark green foliage catching amber light on their upper leaves, silhouetted trunks, dense understory falling away toward the water.

Midground right, the landmark element: a large blue-and-white container ship transiting the canal, seen from behind and slightly to port as it moves away, its hull maybe 250 m long, deck stacked with rows of red, teal and white shipping containers, white superstructure and bridge tower near the bow-forward island, red boot-topping at the waterline, small wake spreading across the channel. The ship is realistically proportioned: it fills the width of the narrow green-banked channel and reads as monumental against the surrounding jungle, its stacked containers rising only a modest fraction of the height of the forested hills behind it. Jungle-covered banks with palms crowd both sides of the channel right up to the water.

Background: rolling forested hills and low blue-green mountain ridges receding in atmospheric layers on both sides of the valley, the tallest peak left of center; the waterway thins into a bright silver thread at the center horizon where the sun sets in a warm white-gold glow.

Sky occupies the upper half: deep blue at the top left with cumulus clouds catching cream and peach highlights, transitioning to streaked cirrus bands of vivid orange, coral, salmon and amber across the right and center, with a luminous pale-yellow horizon.

Lighting: low warm backlight from the setting sun at center, long soft shadows across the road, strong rim light on the jaguar and on canopy edges, humid tropical haze softening the distance. Color palette: emerald and olive greens, terracotta red earth, deep teal water with gold reflections, orange and coral sky, cool blue shadows.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, matte-painting quality, rich painterly brushwork, high dynamic range, 16:9 aspect ratio, highly detailed, sharp foreground detail with soft atmospheric depth, wallpaper composition.
