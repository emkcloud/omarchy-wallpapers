A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of French Guiana at golden hour, seen from a high jungle ridge that looks down over equatorial rainforest, a winding coastal road, a muddy tidal river, a Creole village and the Atlantic Ocean, with an Ariane space rocket standing on its launch pad in the distance.

Far background, right: the Atlantic Ocean stretching to the horizon with gentle surf on a pale sandy beach, a small low island with a lighthouse-like white structure offshore, and coconut palms fringing the coast.
