A cinematic 16:9 digital painting of a tropical Caribbean coastline in Puerto Rico at golden hour, seen from a high mountain overlook where a winding coastal highway descends toward the sea. The composition is a wide panoramic vista with strong depth: a rocky foreground ridge, a lush green midground of hills and a crescent bay, and distant blue mountain ranges meeting the open ocean on the horizon.

Foreground: in the lower-left third, a sunlit grassy ridge of red-brown volcanic soil, dry golden grass tufts, ferns and broad tropical leaves runs diagonally from the bottom-left corner up toward the center-left. Perched on the crest of this ridge, roughly one third in from the left edge and slightly above the lower-third line, sits a small endemic coquí tree frog (Eleutherodactylus), bright yellow-green with pale cream belly, large dark round eye with a golden iris, damp glossy skin, sticky toe pads gripping the grassy edge, body turned three-quarters toward the viewer and facing right toward the sea. The frog is realistically scaled — only about 4-6 cm long, no larger than the grass blades and small leaves immediately around it, dwarfed by the palm trunks behind it; it must never look bigger than a rock or a house. It is rendered close to the camera so it reads clearly, catching warm rim light on its back. Tall slender coconut palms lean in from the extreme left edge, their crowns cut by the frame; matching palms lean in from the right edge, silhouetted dark against the glowing sky. In the lower-right foreground a dry rocky slope of ochre and olive scrub rises above the road.

Midground: a two-lane asphalt highway with white edge lines and a faint yellow center line enters at the bottom-center of the frame, curves in a wide S-shape to the left, then sweeps back to the right and disappears among the hills toward the coastal town. The road surface is dark blue-grey with warm sunset reflections, small guardrail posts on its right shoulder. Along and below the road, dense tropical vegetation: coconut palms of varied heights, banana and breadfruit foliage, flamboyán trees, hedges of green shrub. A small Puerto Rican coastal town spreads across the midground center and center-left, hugging the beach: dozens of low one- and two-storey houses with flat and pitched roofs in terracotta red, cream, pastel yellow, aqua and white, plus a modest whitewashed Spanish-colonial church with a square bell tower and a small cross near the center-left of the settlement. All buildings are small at this distance — a house should read as a few pixels tall relative to the sweeping bay.

Background: a wide crescent bay of luminous turquoise and aquamarine water in the center-right, with a pale sand beach edged by white breaking surf tracing a long arc from the center toward the right. Beyond it a rocky green headland juts into the sea at center-right. On the left and center, layered green mountain ridges of the Cordillera Central recede into hazy blue, forested and rounded, with scattered clearings and tiny distant houses. On the far horizon, faint blue mountain silhouettes and the open Atlantic stretch to the right edge, where the sun sits just above the water, casting a long shimmering gold path across the sea.

Lighting and atmosphere: late golden-hour sunset, sun low on the right horizon, warm amber and peach light raking across the ridges from the right, long soft shadows falling left, cool blue-violet shade in the valleys. Sky occupies the top half: deep cerulean blue at the upper left grading to gold, apricot and cream near the sun on the right, with towering cumulus clouds lit orange along their upper edges on the left and swirling wispy cirrus streaks curling across the upper right. Palette of turquoise, emerald and jade greens, warm ochre and terracotta, deep cerulean, amber and rose-gold. Humid tropical air, gentle atmospheric haze on distant ridges, calm serene mood.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital matte painting, painterly brushwork with crisp detail, high dynamic range, soft volumetric light, wide-angle vista, 16:9 aspect ratio, highly detailed, wallpaper quality, 4K.
