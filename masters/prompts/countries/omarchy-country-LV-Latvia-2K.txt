A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a summer golden-hour landscape in Latvia, in the Gauja River valley of the Vidzeme region, a gentle low-lying country with no mountains — only soft forested ridges and river bluffs. Warm evening light rakes across green meadows, birch groves and a wide slow river.

Foreground, lower right and center: a smooth two-lane asphalt country road with a faded white center line and pale edge markings sweeps into the frame from the bottom center-right, curving in a long S-bend to the right and then away toward the middle distance, where it narrows and disappears past a wooden fence line and a row of small roadside trees. The road occupies roughly the lower right third of the frame; short mown grass verges, wildflowers and a few thin white-painted roadside posts border it. Lower left foreground: a lush green meadow with a small still pond edged by reeds; two large lichen-covered granite glacial boulders sit at the far left edge of the pond, each about knee-to-waist height compared with a person. A single European beaver, correctly sized (about 90 cm long, small against the boulders), swims and pushes at the water's edge just left of center in the pond, leaving faint ripples.

Left of center, midground on the grass between the pond and the road: two white storks (Ciconia ciconia), Latvia's emblematic bird, standing upright about 1 meter tall — realistically small compared with the nearby wooden houses, roughly one-fifth the height of a cottage wall. White bodies, black wing feathers, long red legs and long red-orange beaks. On the right side of the frame, in the near foreground, a tall wooden utility pole rises the full height of the right edge with a large untidy stick nest platform on top; one adult white stork stands in the nest in crisp silhouette against the bright sky, small relative to the pole and the distant castle.

Left midground: a small traditional Latvian rural hamlet — four or five low log and whitewashed cottages with steep grey shingled and thatched-looking gable roofs, timber walls, small windows, a couple of barns and a woodshed, all modest one-story buildings. Just behind them, a slender white village church with a narrow shingled steeple and a small spire rises maybe twice the height of the cottages, still small in the frame. Slim white-barked birch trees with fluttering green leaves frame the far left edge of the image, tall and vertical, together with a scattering of birches in the middle distance.

Center and right midground: the wide calm river winds in from the far distance, curving from center-right toward the viewer, its glassy surface mirroring the orange sky and the dark forest. A pale sandstone cliff with reddish outcrops and a small sandy bank rises on the right bank of the river, about 25–30 meters high, topped with dense green deciduous trees.

Right background, standing on that wooded sandstone bluff: a medieval red-brick castle in the style of Turaida — a tall round donjon tower with a conical dark-tiled roof, adjoining rectangular brick halls with steep red tiled gables, small towers and remnants of stone curtain wall. It is clearly a distant building, occupying about one sixth of the frame's width and height, its brickwork glowing warm orange in the low sun.

Background across the whole horizon: dense unbroken boreal-mixed forest of dark spruce and pine mixed with lighter deciduous crowns, layered in receding blue-grey bands over very gentle low hills; the horizon is essentially flat, with no mountains anywhere.

Lighting and atmosphere: late golden hour, the sun sitting just above the treeline in the center-right, casting a warm amber and peach glow along the horizon that fades into a clear cerulean blue sky in the upper left; large soft cumulus clouds and thin wispy cirrus streaks are lit orange and cream on their undersides. Long soft shadows across the meadow, gentle haze in the distance, mirror-like reflections on the river and pond, a calm, peaceful, pastoral Northern European summer mood.

Color palette: emerald and lime greens, birch white, warm terracotta brick, weathered grey timber, amber and peach sky, deep teal-blue forest shadows.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, matte-painting quality, 16:9 aspect ratio, ultra detailed, natural realistic proportions, wide-angle landscape composition, soft volumetric golden light, high dynamic range.
