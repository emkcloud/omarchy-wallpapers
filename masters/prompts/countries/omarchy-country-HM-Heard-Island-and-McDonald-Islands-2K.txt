A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of Heard Island and McDonald Islands, the remote uninhabited subantarctic Australian territory in the southern Indian Ocean, at golden hour. The composition is dominated by a single massive glaciated volcanic cone rising directly out of the ocean in the center-background: Big Ben with its summit Mawson Peak, a broad symmetrical shield volcano whose upper third is sheathed in thick white glacial ice and snowfields, with blue-tinted crevassed glacier tongues spilling down its central couloirs toward the sea. Dark basaltic ridges, scree spurs and lava buttresses cut through the snow in chocolate-brown and near-black bands, and a faint thin plume of pale volcanic steam drifts from the summit crater into the sky. The mountain occupies roughly the middle 45 percent of the frame width and rises about two thirds of the frame height, utterly dwarfing everything else; its lower flanks fall away in tundra-brown moraine slopes patched with old snow that meet the coastline.

Midground: a wide sweeping black volcanic sand and gravel beach curves in a broad crescent from the left-center to the right, with pale turquoise-white surf breaking in long lines against it. To the left, the cold steel-blue Southern Ocean stretches to the horizon, dotted with two or three small white icebergs and growlers; a tall dark sea stack about 60 meters high stands in the water at the far left, roughly one eighth of the frame height, capped with a thin crust of green-brown moss, with a low chain of smaller skerries and rock islets behind it. Mirrored on the right, a smaller cluster of jagged sea stacks sits in the sunlit water near the right edge, and a second shallow bay with scattered brash ice curves off to the right. There is no architecture, no town and no human structure anywhere in the scene: the island is pristine and uninhabited.

Foreground: a narrow two-lane asphalt road with a solid white edge line and a double yellow center line enters from the bottom-right corner, sweeps in a long S-curve toward the left and center, then straightens and runs away into the distance along the base of the volcano, becoming a thin thread before disappearing into the coastal moraine. The wet asphalt is polished by low sun and mirrors warm amber and orange highlights along its length. Both verges are lined with low subantarctic tundra: dense golden-green tussock grass, cushion plants, Kerguelen cabbage rosettes, wind-scoured basalt boulders and small patches of unmelted snow on the right-hand embankment.

Animal: on the grassy bluff at the lower left, standing on the tussock verge above the beach, three king penguins in realistic scale — each about 90 cm tall, roughly knee-to-hip height of an adult human, clearly much smaller than the road is wide and minuscule against the mountain. The nearest bird stands upright in three-quarter view facing right, its silver-grey back, white belly, black head and vivid orange-yellow ear patches and throat blaze catching the low golden sun; a second and third penguin stand slightly behind and to its right, one smaller and turned away. They occupy only the lower-left twelfth of the frame and cast long thin shadows across the grass.

Lighting and atmosphere: late golden-hour sunset, the sun low and just out of frame to the right, flooding the right side of the sky with warm apricot, amber and pale gold, while the left side of the sky stays cool cobalt and slate blue with scattered cumulus and altocumulus clouds lit orange on their undersides. Rim light traces the volcano's right-hand ridges; the snowfields glow warm cream on the sunward side and cool lavender-blue in shadow. Long soft shadows, crisp cold clean air, faint sea haze on the horizon, subtle spray mist over the surf. Color palette: cold blues and slate greys against warm amber, gold and copper, with the black volcanic sand and dark basalt providing deep contrast.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, matte-painting quality, wide-angle vista, 16:9 aspect ratio, ultra highly detailed, volumetric light, dramatic natural composition, sharp foreground detail with atmospheric depth falloff, 4K wallpaper.
