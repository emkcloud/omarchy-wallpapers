A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a Norwegian coastal fjord landscape in the Lofoten islands at golden hour, seen from a high grassy ridge looking down and out over the sea. Norway's identity is carried by the classic red-painted timber rorbuer fishing cabins with white trim, the deep blue fjord dotted with skerries, the sharp granite peaks plunging straight into the water, and the low midnight-sun light grazing across everything.

FOREGROUND (bottom third): a steep grassy headland covered in golden-green summer grass, low heather and clusters of weathered grey granite outcrops and lichen-covered boulders. In the bottom-left corner and along the left edge, large rounded rock slabs and a few small dark spruce and juniper bushes; a single slender conifer stands on the far left about one-tenth of the frame height. In the bottom-right corner and the entire right edge, a big sloping shelf of bare, cracked grey-pink granite with sparse tufts of grass, angled toward the viewer and catching warm rim light. A narrow two-lane asphalt road with faint white edge markings enters from the lower-center-right, curves in a long lazy S up and to the left across the green slope, passes the village and disappears behind the hill; the road is small in the frame — barely wider than a car — reinforcing the scale of the landscape. Tiny white roadside marker posts line its outer edge.

MIDGROUND: on the left-of-center grassy slope, standing on the ridge just above the road, a single adult red deer stag in warm brown coat with modest branching antlers, seen in profile facing right, realistically scaled — its body is roughly the height of a person, clearly much smaller than any of the cabins below and no taller than the roadside grass ridge; it occupies only a small fraction of the frame. Below and to the right of it, a compact fishing village of about fifteen to twenty red wooden cabins and boathouses with dark grey slate and grass roofs, some raised on wooden stilts over the shallow turquoise water, a small jetty and moored boats; a few white houses scattered further back. The village sits on a low rocky spit that reaches into the fjord in the center of the frame. Small green birch and rowan trees cluster between the buildings. Flat rocky islets and skerries with low vegetation break the water to the right of the village.

BACKGROUND: a wide calm fjord filling the right half of the frame, its surface almost mirror-smooth with soft ripples. Beyond the village on the left and center, a dramatic range of dark grey jagged granite mountains with knife-edge ridges and steep scree faces rising perhaps 800–1000 m straight out of the sea, their sunlit western flanks glowing amber while their shadowed sides stay blue-grey. Successive ranges recede toward the right in ever paler blue atmospheric layers, ending in faint silhouettes on the horizon. Low on the far right horizon the sun sits just above the sea, a bright warm disc casting a long shimmering golden path across the water toward the viewer.

LIGHT AND ATMOSPHERE: late-evening arctic summer golden hour, warm low-angle sunlight from the right, long soft shadows stretching left across the grass, gentle haze in the distance. Sky occupies the upper 40 percent: pale blue at the top-left with scattered small cumulus clouds lit peach and lavender underneath, dissolving into streaky high cirrus and a broad glowing amber-to-gold gradient around the sun on the right. Color palette: warm gold, amber and peach against cool slate blue, teal water, mossy green grass and the saturated barn-red of the cabins. Calm, serene, expansive, slightly nostalgic mood.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, 16:9 widescreen wallpaper, ultra detailed, crisp textures on rock and grass, natural realistic proportions, soft volumetric light, subtle lens bloom around the sun, high dynamic range, 2K.
