A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a South Korean highland landscape at golden-hour sunset, seen from a high mountain pass road that descends into a wide valley of rolling green ridges and layered blue mountain silhouettes, evoking the Baekdudaegan range and Korean countryside.
