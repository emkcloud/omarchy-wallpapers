Cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of Saint-Barthélemy (St. Barts), French West Indies, seen from a high grassy hillside viewpoint at golden hour, looking down over the Saint-Jean bay, its famous short airstrip and the harbour town of Gustavia beyond.

FOREGROUND (bottom third): a sunlit slope of tall dry-green tropical grass, low sea-grape shrubs, broad glossy banana and philodendron leaves entering from the lower-left and lower-right corners, and clusters of magenta and pink bougainvillea on the left. Lying on a flat rock in the lower-left quadrant, roughly one third of the way in from the left edge, a single adult green iguana (Iguana iguana) in full profile facing right, realistically scaled at about 1.2–1.5 m from snout to tail tip — no larger than the grass tussocks and rock it rests on, tiny compared with the houses behind it, never oversized. Its olive-green scaled body, banded tail, dewlap and dorsal spines are lit warmly from the right, casting a soft shadow on the rock. Two slender coconut palms lean in from the extreme left edge, their crowns silhouetted against the sky; a cluster of palm fronds also frames the far right edge.

MIDGROUND: the hillside descends in overlapping green ridges dotted with small Caribbean-French villas — white and cream stucco walls, bright terracotta red gable roofs, green wooden shutters, small pools and hedges — scattered at low density across both the left ridges and the centre-right slope, each house small in the frame to convey distance and elevation. A narrow two-lane asphalt road winds in tight S-curves down the left-centre ridge from the upper hills toward the coast, passing between the villas, with pale guardrails and a few parked cars, its width no more than a thin ribbon at this scale. At centre-right the land flattens into a single short airport runway (Gustaf III / Saint-Jean airstrip) running left-to-right along the beach, with white threshold markings, a small low terminal building and a windsock beside it. A twin-engine white and blue regional turboprop commuter aircraft (ATR/Twin Otter style, about 20 m wingspan) is on very low final approach just above the ridge at centre-left of the sky, banking slightly, seen from behind and above — small in the frame, clearly smaller than the hill it crosses.

BACKGROUND: a wide crescent of pale golden sand beach on the right of centre, meeting a shallow lagoon of luminous turquoise and aquamarine water that deepens to sapphire blue toward the horizon. Beyond the beach, on a rocky point at right, the red-and-white roofed town of Gustavia hugs a sheltered harbour crowded with dozens of moored white sailing yachts, catamarans and motor yachts of realistic small scale, stone quays and a breakwater. On the headland behind them stands a small white lighthouse with a red lantern housing, and a second slender red-and-white lighthouse tower slightly further left on the ridge, both modest in size. Green volcanic hills roll away on the left and centre-left into blue hazy distance; two small offshore islets and rocky cays sit on the horizon at centre-right in the open Caribbean Sea.

LIGHT AND COLOUR: late-afternoon golden-hour sunset light coming from the right, warm amber and peach glow on the horizon and on the undersides of the clouds, long soft shadows across the grassy foreground, and a deep saturated cobalt-blue sky at the top-left corner. Towering cumulus clouds pile over the hills on the left, wispy cirrus streaks radiate across the upper right, catching pink, coral and cream highlights. Palette: emerald and olive greens, terracotta red roofs, turquoise and teal sea, warm gold, peach and coral sky, deep navy blue upper sky.

ATMOSPHERE: tranquil, luxurious, tropical island serenity; slight haze over the distant sea; crisp air with sun flare near the horizon.

STYLE: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, matte-painting quality, wide-angle elevated vantage point, deep depth of field with atmospheric perspective, 16:9 aspect ratio, ultra-detailed, high dynamic range, natural realistic proportions throughout.
