A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of Macau at golden-hour sunset, seen from a green hillside road on the Macau peninsula looking down over the dense city, the Praia Grande bay and the bridges to Taipa. Wide establishing landscape composition with strong depth: grassy foreground slopes, a compact colonial-and-modern cityscape in the midground, and hazy blue coastal hills and islands on the horizon.

FOREGROUND: a smooth two-lane asphalt road enters from the bottom-center of the frame and sweeps in a wide S-curve to the right, climbing over a low ridge and disappearing toward the city; it occupies roughly the lower quarter of the image, with a crisp white edge line on the left, a dashed white centre line, and a low pale kerb. The wet-looking asphalt catches warm orange reflections of the sunset. Flanking the road are steep grassy embankments: on the left a rocky bank of grey granite boulders, tall sun-lit wild grasses and low shrubs; on the right a similar green bank rising higher, topped with scrub and small broadleaf trees. On the left edge of the frame, a leafy tropical broadleaf tree (banyan-like, about 7-8 m tall) frames the composition; a mirrored cluster of smaller trees and bushes frames the right edge.

ANIMAL: a single small oriental magpie-robin (black-and-white songbird, roughly 20 cm long, realistically tiny — no larger than a human hand) perched on a grey granite rock in the lower-left foreground, in sharp focus, its body in profile facing right toward the city, tail slightly raised. It must read as a small bird on a rock, occupying only a tiny fraction of the frame — never oversized relative to the boulders, grass or road.
