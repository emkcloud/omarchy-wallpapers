A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a northern Algerian coastal landscape at golden hour, seen from a high mountain pass in the Tell Atlas as it descends toward the Mediterranean Sea. The viewpoint is elevated, looking north-east down a long green valley toward a sunlit shoreline in the far distance.

Pale sandy-cream fur rimmed with warm backlight, oversized pointed ears translucent in the sun, slender legs, dark-tipped bushy tail, alert posture.
