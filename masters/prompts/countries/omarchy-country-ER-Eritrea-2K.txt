A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of the Eritrean highland escarpment at golden-hour sunset, looking east from the Asmara plateau down the great escarpment toward the hazy Red Sea coast. The scene reads unmistakably as Eritrea: a narrow-gauge Italian-built colonial railway on a stone arch viaduct, a whitewashed highland Orthodox church above a village of dry-stone houses, an oryx on the dry grassy slope, camels on a village track, acacias and candelabra euphorbia, and terraced tawny-green hills falling away to a distant coastal plain.

FOREGROUND (bottom third): a smooth two-lane asphalt highway enters at the bottom center-right and sweeps in a long S-curve up and away into the middle distance, its faded white centre line and edge lines catching low sun; wet-looking sheen on the tarmac reflects orange sky. The road occupies roughly the lower-right quarter of the frame and narrows to a thin ribbon by the midground. Left and bottom-left: a stony, sun-baked hillside of ochre soil, scattered granite boulders, dry golden tussock grass and low green scrub. On this left slope, about one third in from the left edge and just below the horizon of the ridge, stands a single beisa oryx in profile facing right — a normal-sized antelope roughly the height of the small acacia bushes beside it, its body only a small fraction of the frame (about 6-7% of image height), with long straight ridged horns, pale grey-fawn coat, black facial mask stripes, black flank stripe and black tail. To its left, a low flat-topped umbrella acacia tree, clearly taller than the oryx. Right foreground edge: dry scrub, aloes, tall grasses and another leaning acacia.

MIDGROUND: crossing the left-center of the frame at about 60% height, an elegant multi-arch masonry railway viaduct of pale grey stone with seven to eight round arches, hugging the hillside; a small vintage black steam locomotive with a tall funnel pulls three or four short green-and-cream passenger carriages across it left-to-right, releasing a soft white plume of smoke that drifts up and right. The train is small and correctly scaled — the locomotive no larger than a village house, tiny against the mountain slopes. Its single-track railbed continues out of frame to the left along the hillside contour above the oryx. On the right side of the frame, terraced on a spur above the road, a hillside village of flat-roofed dry-stone and mud-brick houses with rough stone walls, small green garden terraces and footpaths; above and behind them, at roughly two-thirds width and just below the horizon, a modest whitewashed highland church with a triangular pierced bell-gable holding a cross, a second small domed belfry, three arched doorways and pale plastered walls — building-scale, only slightly larger than the surrounding houses. Interspersed among the houses: candelabra euphorbia and dragon-blood-like trees with radiating branch clusters, plus a broad acacia at the far right edge. A tiny caravan of two or three camels with a herder walks a dirt path in front of the village at mid-right, human figures no more than a few pixels tall — clearly small against the houses.

BACKGROUND: rolling green-gold plateau ridges with the winding road reappearing as a bright thread twice in the far valley; layered blue-violet mountain ranges receding on the left and center, a prominent conical peak on the far left horizon; on the right, the escarpment drops into a pale luminous haze over the distant lowlands and the shimmer of the Red Sea and a faint headland on the far horizon.

LIGHT AND COLOR: the sun sits just above the horizon at center-right, a small brilliant white-gold disc casting a warm glowing corridor across the valleys; long soft shadows to the left; rim light on grass, stone walls and the oryx. Sky fills the upper 40% — deep cobalt blue at the top corners with dramatic streaked cirrus and altocumulus clouds lit amber, peach, cream and lavender, radiating outward from the sun. Palette: warm gold, amber, terracotta and ochre in the land; teal-blue and dusty violet in the shadows and distant ranges; soft pastel haze on the coast. Atmosphere: serene, expansive, epic and quiet, with volumetric light and gentle aerial perspective.
