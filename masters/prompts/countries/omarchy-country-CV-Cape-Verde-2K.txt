A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a volcanic island landscape in Cape Verde, on the island of Fogo, at golden hour just before sunset, seen from a high ridge that looks down over black lava fields toward the Atlantic Ocean.

Background, center-left: a large, perfectly conical dark basalt stratovolcano (Pico do Fogo) rising roughly two-thirds of the image height, its symmetrical ash-grey and charcoal slopes streaked with old lava channels, a thin plume of pale white-grey steam drifting from the summit crater to the upper left. Behind and to the left of the cone, a long horizontal caldera escarpment (the Chã das Caldeiras wall) runs off the left edge of the frame, its layered cliff face 300–400 metres tall, lit warm ochre and rust-orange on its upper rim while its base falls into blue shadow. To the right of the volcano the caldera rim continues as a lower flat-topped ridge that steps down toward the sea. In the far right distance, across a calm silver-blue ocean, a small jagged neighbouring island silhouette sits low on the horizon beneath a glowing pale-gold sunset sky. On the right middle distance the land ends in dark cliffs dropping into a small turquoise bay, with a solitary sea stack rising just offshore.

Midground, center-right: a tiny traditional Cape Verdean village nestled on a terrace of the lava plain, small in scale so that its houses read as distant rectangles — whitewashed and pastel-coloured single-storey cube houses with terracotta and blue tin roofs, and at its centre a modest white Portuguese-colonial parish church with a square bell tower and small cross, the tallest building in the village but still only a fraction of the volcano's height. Around the village, small walled fields of green vines and vegetable plots in neat rectangular terraces on the dark volcanic soil, with low dry-stone walls.

Foreground and midground, center: a narrow winding cobblestone road (traditional hand-laid basalt paving) enters at the bottom center of the frame, curves in a wide S-shape up the ridge from lower-center to the left, then sweeps right across the slope and continues past the village toward the coastal cliffs, its grey stones catching warm light and its edge bordered by a low stacked-stone retaining wall.

Foreground left: rough black volcanic boulders and scoria, dry golden tufts of grass, and a cluster of spiky succulents — agave/aloe rosettes with tall flowering stalks — plus a small wind-shaped dragon-tree-like shrub in the upper-left corner, all sharply detailed and in warm rim light.

Foreground right: a small herd of two domestic goats standing on the grassy rocky verge beside the road — one adult brown-and-tan goat in profile with short curved horns and a beard, about 70 cm tall at the shoulder, no larger than the nearby grass tussocks and clearly much smaller than the distant houses; a young kid a few metres behind it and further away, correspondingly smaller. Their scale is strictly realistic: knee-high animals on a vast volcanic ridge.

Palette of charcoal black, warm ochre, burnt sienna, olive-gold grass, whitewashed village white, and turquoise-to-slate ocean blues.
