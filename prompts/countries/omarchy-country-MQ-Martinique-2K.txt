A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of Martinique in the French West Indies at golden hour: a winding coastal road hugging a cliff above a calm turquoise Caribbean bay, with a colorful Creole seaside town spread along the shore and a large green volcanic cone rising behind it, evoking Saint-Pierre and Le Carbet beneath Mont Pelee. Camera low and centered on the asphalt, wide-angle, horizon slightly below the middle of the frame.

FOREGROUND (bottom third): a two-lane asphalt coastal road enters from the bottom center, sweeps in a wide S-curve to the right and climbs out of frame toward the middle right; fresh dark-grey tarmac with a broken white center line and solid white edge lines, subtle wet sheen catching warm backlight. Along the left edge of the road, a low weathered stone-block parapet wall about 80 cm high runs diagonally from the bottom-left corner toward the center, topped by short white guard posts; tufts of coarse tropical grass and small green weeds sprout at its base and between the stones. Perched on the top of this wall in the lower-left area, a single small yellow songbird (a yellow-breasted Antillean warbler / bananaquit-type bird, only about 12 cm long, no larger than one stone block, roughly 1/40 of the frame height) stands in profile facing right, olive-yellow back, bright lemon-yellow breast, thin dark beak, warm rim light along its back — realistically tiny compared to the wall and the road. On the right side of the road, a dark basalt rock cutting and steep vegetated embankment with a yellow-and-black chevron road sign on a slim post, plus a slim white marker post further along.

MIDGROUND: the road curves past a stone-and-concrete sea wall and a short coastal causeway into the town. A small Creole colonial town occupies the center-right shoreline: two- and three-storey pastel houses (cream, mint, pale pink, pale blue) with red and dark-grey pitched metal roofs, wooden shutters, verandas, balconies and fretwork balustrades; a few small cars parked along the seafront street; scattered coconut palms 12–18 m tall standing above the rooflines. Slightly right of center stands the town's stone Catholic church, the only tall building: a pale limestone facade with an arched portal, a square bell tower topped by a slender reddish-brown pointed spire, its total height roughly three to four times a neighbouring two-storey house but still very small against the mountain behind it. On the far right foreground/midground edge, tall coconut palms lean over the road from a dark green vegetated bluff, with a large Creole villa with a red roof and white veranda on the ridge.

BACKGROUND: a wide crescent bay of calm, glassy water on the left half, shading from pale turquoise near the dark volcanic-sand beach to deeper blue toward the left frame edge; gentle white foam lines curving along the shoreline. On the left, a low green headland with rocky cliffs juts into the sea. Dominating the center and rising directly behind the town, a single massive volcanic cone: broad steep symmetrical flanks clothed in dense emerald rainforest, bare brown-grey rock and ravines near the darker summit, cloud shadow on its left slope, its summit reaching roughly the upper third of the frame and dwarfing the town at its foot. To its right, lower forested ridges and hillsides dotted with tiny scattered houses fade into golden haze.

LIGHTING AND PALETTE: late-afternoon golden hour, the sun low and just right of the volcano, hidden behind the ridge, casting a warm glow, long soft shadows and strong warm rim light on the road, the wall, the palms and the bird. Sky occupies the upper half: soft blue at top left with large cumulus clouds lit peach, gold and cream, deepening to warm amber and honey near the sun on the right. Palette of turquoise and cerulean sea, emerald and olive foliage, warm amber and gold light, pastel Creole facades, terracotta roofs, dark basalt rock. Humid tropical atmosphere with light aerial haze over the distant ridges.

STYLE: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, wallpaper composition, 16:9, ultra detailed, natural realistic proportions, sharp foreground detail, soft atmospheric depth, high dynamic range, no text, no people in the foreground.
