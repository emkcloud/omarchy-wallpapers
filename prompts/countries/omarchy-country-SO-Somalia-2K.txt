A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of the Somali coast at golden hour, where the arid Horn of Africa desert meets the Indian Ocean. The scene is a wide elevated panorama looking down a gently sloping shoulder of rust-orange sand toward a vast pale beach and a turquoise sea on the right half of the frame.

Foreground, lower half: warm ochre and terracotta sand dunes with wind-carved ripples, scattered dry tussocks of pale green desert grass, small dark pebbles and low thorny scrub bushes clustered along the lower-left and lower-center edges. A narrow unpaved sandy track, roughly two vehicle widths, enters at the bottom-center of the frame and winds in a long lazy S-curve up and to the right, its parallel tire ruts catching the low sun before it thins to a faint pale line and disappears among the dunes in the middle distance. The track occupies only a small fraction of the frame's width, reading as a modest desert trail, not a highway.

Left side, foreground-to-midground: a single flat-topped umbrella acacia tree standing on a dune crest at the far left edge, about 6-7 meters tall, its dark green canopy silhouetted against the hazy hills behind it. On the slope just right of it, in the left third of the frame and set slightly back, a single adult dromedary camel (one hump, tan-brown coat, long neck, slender legs) stands in profile facing right, browsing. The camel is realistically scaled: roughly 2 meters at the hump, clearly much smaller than the acacia beside it and no larger than a small car, its body occupying only a modest portion of the dune it stands on, with a long soft shadow stretching left across the sand. A few smaller acacias, 4-5 meters tall, dot the mid-ground dunes at center and center-right, diminishing with distance.

Right side, midground: a small traditional Somali nomadic encampment of three or four aqal Somali huts - low dome-shaped shelters woven from bent sticks and covered with mats, hides and cloth, each only about 2 meters high and 3-4 meters wide, one with a dark low doorway facing the viewer. Thin wooden poles and drying frames lean beside them. The huts sit on the flat sandy terrace above the beach, dwarfed by the openness of the landscape and clearly smaller than the acacia trees near them. A larger acacia stands at the far right edge behind the camp.

Midground to background, right and center: a long crescent of blindingly pale white sand beach curving away into the distance, with a thin line of white surf, then bands of shallow luminous turquoise and aquamarine water deepening to steel blue toward the horizon. The ocean fills the entire right third and the far center of the frame, meeting the sky at a crisp flat horizon.

Background, left and center-left: layered ranges of low arid hills and rocky escarpments in dusty mauve and blue-grey, softened by atmospheric haze, receding in three or four planes toward the coastline.

Lighting and atmosphere: late-afternoon golden hour, the sun low and just above the sea near the right edge, casting a shimmering pale-gold reflection path across the water and raking warm light across the dunes so every ripple has a long shadow. Sky occupies the upper half: deep blue at the top-left with large billowing cumulus clouds lit peach, cream and apricot on their undersides, thinning into a smooth warm gold-and-cream gradient above the sun on the right. Colour palette of burnt orange, terracotta, warm sand, cream, sage green, turquoise and dusty blue. Calm, spacious, silent, dry heat with a faint sea breeze.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, matte-painting quality, soft painterly brushwork with crisp detail, wide-angle vista, 16:9 aspect ratio, highly detailed, natural realistic proportions, volumetric golden light, subtle atmospheric haze, no text, no people in close-up.
