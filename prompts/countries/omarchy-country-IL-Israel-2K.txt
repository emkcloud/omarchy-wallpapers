A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a golden-hour panorama over Jerusalem, Israel, seen from the limestone hills of the Judean highlands looking west toward the Old City. The viewpoint sits high on a rocky ridge, like the Mount of Olives slope, with a winding asphalt road descending from the lower-right foreground into the valley.
