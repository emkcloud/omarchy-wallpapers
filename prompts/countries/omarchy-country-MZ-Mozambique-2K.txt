A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a tropical Indian Ocean coastline in Mozambique at golden hour, seen from a slightly elevated viewpoint on top of low white sand dunes. In the immediate foreground, occupying the bottom third of the frame from edge to edge, wide rolling dunes of pale cream-white powder sand, sculpted by wind into soft ridges and shallow hollows, their surfaces marked by fine ripples and scattered footprints; tufts of stiff olive-green and straw-yellow dune grass grow in irregular clumps along the left edge, along the right side and across the crest of the central dune, each clump about knee height. A wide sandy track, made of pale packed sand with faint parallel vehicle ruts, enters at the bottom center of the frame and winds in a long S-curve up and to the left, then bends back to the right, narrowing with distance until it dissolves into the beach flats in the midground; the road is roughly the width of two vehicles in the foreground and shrinks to a thin pale ribbon at the midground.

On the left side of the midground, at about one third of the frame height and clearly smaller than the dune ridge in front of it, a single adult hippopotamus rests on the flat damp sand beside a shallow coastal estuary channel, lying on its side with legs folded, its body correctly proportioned: roughly the length of a small car and only about half the height of the nearby thatched roofs, its wet grey-mauve hide catching warm pink-orange rim light along the back and flank, with darker slate shadow underneath. Behind the hippo, a small coastal fishing village runs along the left third of the midground: eight to ten low round and square huts with steep conical palm-thatch roofs and pale reed or plaster walls, each hut single storey and roughly three times the hippo's height at the ridge, arranged in a loose line receding into the distance and diminishing in size with perspective. Behind and among the huts, a dense grove of tall coconut palms fills the left edge and left background, their slender curving trunks and feathered fronds silhouetted against the sky, the tallest palms in the left foreground about four times the height of the huts; the palm line continues in smaller and hazier form toward the center of the frame where it meets the horizon.

In the center and right midground, the beach curves gently away in a long crescent, the wet sand mirroring the sky in soft peach and gold, meeting a calm turquoise and aquamarine sea with pale foam lines of low breaking waves. On the right, floating in shallow clear water about one third in from the right edge and at eye level with the horizon, a traditional wooden dhow sailing boat with a dark varnished hull and a single tall triangular lateen sail of weathered off-white canvas leaning to the left, its mast raked forward; the boat is modest in scale, about the length of the fishing huts are wide, its sail rising to roughly half the frame height, small enough to read as a real fishing vessel and not a ship. Far in the background on the right, low hazy blue-grey island silhouettes and a long flat headland sit on the horizon, small and distant. The sun sits just above the horizon slightly right of center, a bright warm glow bleaching the sky to pale gold and amber near the water line.
