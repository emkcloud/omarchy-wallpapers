A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a New Caledonia coastal landscape at golden hour, seen from a high grassy headland looking down over the vast turquoise lagoon of Grande Terre and its distant barrier reef. Elevated three-quarter viewpoint, wide panoramic depth from a close foreground slope to far mountain ranges on the horizon.

Foreground, lower left and left third: a steep sunlit hillside of dry golden-green savannah grass, low scrub, yellow wildflowers and exposed red-brown lateritic soil and rock, framed by two dark slender columnar pines (Araucaria columnaris, New Caledonia pines) rising along the extreme left edge, their narrow spire silhouettes about three times the height of a person. Standing on this grassy slope in the left foreground, roughly one third up from the bottom edge and in sharp focus, a single kagu (Rhynochetos jubatus), the flightless endemic bird of New Caledonia: pale ash-grey and white plumage, loose shaggy crest raised on the back of its head, bright orange-red beak and long orange-red legs, body about the size of a domestic chicken (around 55 cm long, knee-high to a person) — realistically small, no larger than the surrounding shrubs and clearly tiny compared with the houses far below. It stands in profile facing right toward the sea, warm rim light catching its crest and back, casting a small soft shadow on the grass.

Midground, center and lower center-right: a narrow two-lane asphalt coastal road with a faded white center line, entering from the bottom center-right of the frame and snaking in a wide S-curve up and to the left along the ridge, its wet-looking dark surface reflecting warm amber sky light, bordered by short grass verges, a few white guardrail posts on the left bend, and green shrubs. The road width is modest — about two car widths — and it narrows convincingly as it climbs toward the far coast.

Midground right of the road and along the shoreline: a small scattered seaside village of low single-storey houses with pale walls and red, blue and grey pitched metal roofs, plus a couple of simple round Kanak-style thatched huts with conical roofs among them, all only one or two storeys tall and dwarfed by the landscape. Tall coconut palms and broad flame trees cluster between the houses; a thin white sand beach curves along the water. More green rolling hills with pine-dotted ridges on the right side of the frame, sloping down to the lagoon.

Background: an immense calm lagoon filling the right half and center of the image, banded from pale mint and aquamarine over the shallow sand flats to deep sapphire blue beyond, with visible dark coral patch reefs under the clear water and a long thin white line of breaking surf marking the outer barrier reef stretching across the middle distance. Far left and along the horizon, a chain of low blue-violet hazy mountains fading into atmospheric perspective; a tiny islet silhouette near the horizon on the right.

Lighting: late golden-hour sunset, the low sun sitting just above the sea on the right horizon, throwing a long shimmering golden path across the water and warm amber-peach light across the grass, the road and the bird, while the left hillside falls into cooler blue-green shadow. Sky filled with sweeping wind-brushed cirrus streaks, blue at the upper left turning gold, apricot and soft pink toward the sun.

Color palette: turquoise and sapphire lagoon, warm gold and olive grass, deep pine green, red-earth accents, amber and peach sky, cool blue distant mountains.

Atmosphere: serene, tropical, expansive, gently hazy sea air, tranquil island evening.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, painterly brushwork with crisp detail, 16:9 aspect ratio, ultra wide landscape, highly detailed, volumetric golden light, soft atmospheric haze, wallpaper quality, 2K.
