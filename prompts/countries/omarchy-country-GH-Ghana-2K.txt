A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a golden-hour coastal landscape in Ghana, West Africa, on the Gulf of Guinea, where green savanna hills meet a warm turquoise Atlantic bay. Composition split diagonally: a grassy headland occupies the left and lower-left, a curving asphalt road sweeps in from the bottom-center, and a whitewashed colonial sea fort sits on a small rocky promontory in the right-of-center middle distance above a pale sand beach.

Foreground (bottom center and bottom right): a two-lane tarmac road enters at the bottom edge, wide and close to the camera, its worn grey-blue asphalt marked with a faded white edge line and a broken center line; it curves in a long S-bend to the right and then narrows toward the coastline, shrinking to a thin ribbon before disappearing behind low coastal scrub. Along the left verge, tussocks of tall golden-green elephant grass, small ferns and low shrubs lean over the shoulder; scattered weathered grey granite boulders, roughly knee- to waist-height, sit half-buried in the slope. On the right verge, dense bright green grass and reeds catch rim light.

Left midground: a steep grassy hillside rising toward the upper left, its slope covered in sunlit yellow-green grass with soft wind-combed texture. Standing on the crest of this ridge, in profile facing right, is a single adult African bush elephant (Loxodonta africana) with large fan-shaped ears, curved ivory tusks and a lowered trunk. It is realistically scaled: about 3 metres at the shoulder, clearly smaller than the umbrella-crowned acacia trees behind it and only a fraction of the height of the distant fort — a distinct but modest silhouette occupying roughly one eighth of the image height, dark grey-brown skin rimmed with warm sunset light. Behind and left of the elephant, a flat-topped acacia and a slender coconut palm break the ridgeline at the far left edge.

Midground center-right: a strip of coastal savanna with scattered palms, low acacias and pale sandy patches between the road and the shore. On the beach at right-center, six to eight long narrow wooden Ghanaian fishing canoes (pirogues) are drawn up on the sand, brightly painted in red, blue and yellow, small in scale beside the wide beach, some with thin masts and coiled nets.

Background right of center: the landmark — a large whitewashed West African coastal castle in the manner of Elmina/Cape Coast Castle: a low, massive rectangular fortress with lime-washed white walls, crenellated battlements, square corner bastions, a taller central keep with small dark rectangular windows, and a flagstaff. It stands on a low rock outcrop that drops directly to the sea, sunlit on its left face, occupying about a fifth of the frame width and clearly monumental compared to the canoes and the elephant. To its right, a calm turquoise bay curves toward a hazy horizon, with gentle white surf lines rolling onto the sand and a single tall coconut palm at the far right edge of the frame. No mountains — the horizon is flat ocean.

Sky and light: two-thirds of the frame is a dramatic late-afternoon sky, deep blue at the upper left fading to peach, apricot and pale gold near the horizon behind the fort, where the low sun glows just above the sea. Long sweeping cirrus and windswept altocumulus streaks radiate diagonally across the sky in creamy white and lavender, brush-stroked and swirling. Warm golden light rakes across the grass and the elephant's back from the right; cool blue-violet shadows fill the left slope.

Color palette: warm gold, amber and peach in the sky and grass; deep cobalt and slate blue in the upper sky and shadows; emerald and olive greens on the headland; bone-white architecture; turquoise and aquamarine sea.

Atmosphere: serene, expansive, tropical West African coast at sunset, gentle sea breeze, soft haze on the horizon.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, painterly brushwork in the clouds, wide-angle vista, 16:9 aspect ratio, ultra detailed, high dynamic range, wallpaper composition with the road leading the eye toward the castle.
