A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of the raised coral-atoll coastline of Niue in the South Pacific at golden-hour sunset, seen from a slightly elevated clifftop viewpoint looking west over the open ocean. The land is a low uplifted limestone (makatea) terrace, not a volcanic mountain island: pale grey-white, deeply weathered and pitted coral rock, sharp karst ridges, fissured boulders and shallow tide pools, dotted with tufts of coarse green grass, low salt-tolerant shrubs and a few small pandanus and coastal ferns clinging in the cracks.

Foreground, lower-left quadrant: a large rounded slab of eroded coral limestone about one metre high, its surface rough and honeycombed, catching warm side light. Standing on top of that boulder, in profile facing right toward the camera path, is a single coconut crab (uga, Birgus latro) — Niue's emblematic land crab — realistically sized at roughly 60-90 cm across the outstretched legs, so it is clearly much smaller than the boulder it stands on and only a small fraction of the height of the cliff behind it, never as big as a vehicle or a building. Its carapace is deep reddish-brown and purple with fine ridged texture, one enlarged claw raised slightly, long thin antennae backlit by the low sun, thin rim-light along its legs and a soft cast shadow on the rock. Bottom-left corner and lower edge: more grey karst rock, small green sprigs, and dark crevices in shadow.

Midground, centre and centre-right: a narrow two-lane asphalt coastal road with a faded white edge line curves in an elegant S from the bottom-right corner of the frame, sweeping left and then bending back to the left-centre before disappearing behind the coral bluffs. The road occupies roughly the lower third of the frame; its wet-looking dark surface reflects orange and gold sunlight in long streaks. Its verges are thin strips of grass and low scrub against exposed limestone. No vehicles, no people.

Right-of-centre midground: a broad shelf of flat coral reef rock with clear shallow tidal pools reflecting the sky, small ledges and dark undercut notches where the sea has eaten the limestone, and gentle white surf breaking over the fringing reef.

Left and centre background: a chain of layered white-grey limestone sea cliffs and headlands, roughly 20-30 metres tall, stepping away into the distance with green scrub caps on their flat tops and small sheltered coves of vivid turquoise water at their base — one bright aquamarine bay in the centre-left contrasting the deep blue ocean. Far right and centre distance: two or three small flat-topped coral sea stacks and an islet rising just above the water line, small relative to the horizon.

Background: an immense calm Pacific Ocean filling the upper-right and centre, deep cobalt to sapphire blue with subtle wave texture, and a low sun sitting just above the horizon at centre-right, laying a long shimmering golden glitter path across the water.
