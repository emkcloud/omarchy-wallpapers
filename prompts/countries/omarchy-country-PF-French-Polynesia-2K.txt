A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of French Polynesia at golden hour: a dramatic South Pacific high volcanic island of the Society Islands, its jagged basalt spires wrapped in dense emerald jungle, rising straight out of a glowing turquoise lagoon, with a narrow coastal road curving along the shoreline and a dolphin leaping from the deep blue water.

Foreground, lower right and right edge: lush tropical vegetation forms a dark framing mass occupying roughly the right quarter of the frame — broad glossy heliconia and hibiscus leaves, ferns and red tropical blossoms, with three or four tall slender coconut palms whose crowns reach the top-right corner, their trunks leaning slightly seaward and backlit by the low sun so the fronds glow amber-green. Foreground, bottom center to bottom right: a smooth two-lane asphalt coastal road enters from the bottom edge of the frame, sweeps left-to-right in a wide S-curve and recedes into the midground palm grove; the wet, freshly rained asphalt is dark blue-grey with long mirror-like reflections of the orange sky and warm white specular streaks running along the curve. The road is bordered on its seaward (left) side by a low grey stone-and-concrete parapet wall about knee-to-waist height, capped with pale slabs, following the curve of the beach; on its inland (right) side by a thin white painted edge line and a green grassy verge with shrubs. The road occupies the lower right third of the composition and is realistically scaled — roughly 6–7 meters wide, dwarfed by the mountains behind it.

Left foreground and midground, occupying the left third: the open ocean and lagoon. In the deep sapphire-blue water at lower left, about one third up from the bottom edge, a single bottlenose dolphin breaches in mid-leap, arcing left-to-back, its wet grey-silver body catching the golden light, with a small white splash and spray trail beneath it. The dolphin is realistically small in the scene — roughly 2.5 meters long, only a few percent of the frame width, clearly tiny compared to the beach, palms and mountains, never oversized. Behind it the water transitions in bands from deep navy and cobalt to brilliant electric turquoise and pale aquamarine over the shallow coral shelf, with gentle rolling swells and thin white foam lines.

Midground center: a wide crescent of pale golden-white sand beach curving from center-left to the right, met by soft translucent turquoise waves and a delicate lace of foam. Behind the beach a dense belt of coconut palms and tropical greenery runs across the middle of the frame, individual palms small and human-scaled, with a few scattered pastel roofs of a small village barely visible among the trees near center-left, tiny in the distance. A low green forested headland/peninsula juts into the lagoon at center-left.

Background, filling the upper center and center-right: the towering volcanic massif — two dominant peaks. The larger, right of center, is a sharp pyramidal horn about 900 meters tall, its steep flanks fluted with erosion ridges, cloaked in vivid green forest with exposed dark basalt cliffs and crags, its sunlit western face glowing warm gold while its left flank falls into cool blue shadow. To its left, a serrated ridge of needle-like pinnacles and knife-edge crests, slightly lower, also jungle-covered. Further right, softer secondary ridges fade into haze. Far left background: small low motu islets and a distant hazy blue-green island silhouette sitting on the horizon line, tiny by comparison, with the flat ocean horizon visible at the far left.

Lighting and sky: late-afternoon golden hour, the sun low and just out of frame behind the palms on the right, casting warm rim light on the peaks, palm crowns and wave crests. The upper sky is a clear deep cerulean blue at the top-left, transitioning to peach, apricot and pale gold near the horizon on the right; scattered cumulus and wispy altocumulus clouds drift across the upper half, their undersides lit rose-orange, their tops cool white and blue-grey. Soft atmospheric haze separates the mountain layers.

Color palette: emerald and jade greens, deep basalt greys, turquoise and cobalt blues, warm amber, apricot and gold highlights, cream sand. Mood: serene, tropical, expansive, paradisiacal, cinematic.

Style: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, matte-painting quality, wide-angle lens feel, 16:9 aspect ratio, ultra detailed textures on foliage, water and wet asphalt, volumetric light, natural realistic proportions throughout, 2K wallpaper composition with clean uncluttered depth layering.
