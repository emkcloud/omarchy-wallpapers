A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a Mediterranean Riviera coastline at golden-hour sunset, evoking the principality of Monaco and its Côte d'Azur setting. The composition is built around a winding coastal corniche road and a fortified palace standing on a rocky promontory above the sea.

Foreground, lower center and lower right: a two-lane asphalt corniche road with faded white center dashes enters from the bottom edge slightly right of center, sweeps in a wide S-curve to the left, then bends back to the right and climbs toward the promontory, disappearing behind rock. The road occupies roughly the lower fifth of the frame, is realistically narrow (about two car widths, no vehicles on it), and is bordered on its seaward side by a low pale stone parapet wall that follows every curve. A small red-and-white road sign on a slim pole stands on the left verge at mid-left of the curve, no taller than a person.

Foreground left and lower left: a grassy, sun-warmed hillside slope with clumps of dry golden grass, low green shrubs, and scattered weathered limestone boulders and rock outcrops, filling the left third of the lower frame and framing the road. Small maritime pines and rounded Mediterranean shrubs (parasol pines, cypress-like conifers, olive-toned scrub) line the road's inner bank in the midground; the pines are ordinary tree scale, roughly three to five times human height, clearly much smaller than the buildings behind them. A single larger umbrella pine with a broad flat canopy stands at the far left edge, mid-height in the frame, silhouetted against the bay.

Midground right: a tall rugged limestone headland — "the Rock" — rises straight out of the deep blue sea, its cliff face broken into warm ochre and grey ledges with green vegetation in the cracks, with small wave-washed rock stacks and white foam at its base on the right. On its flat summit sits a compact fortified palace-castle in pale cream stone with terracotta red-tiled roofs: a square crenellated keep tower with a thin flagpole on top at its left side, a second smaller battlemented tower, long two-storey wings with tall arched and mullioned windows, some already lit warm amber from inside, and lower defensive walls hugging the cliff edge. The palace is realistically sized — roughly three to four storeys tall, its whole footprint far smaller than the rock it crowns, occupying about one sixth of the frame width, positioned right of center at mid-height.

Midground left and center: a wide calm bay of deep blue-violet water with soft mirror reflections of the sky, curving inward. Along the far shore of the bay, a dense low-rise Mediterranean town of pastel apartment blocks and villas — cream, apricot, terracotta and pale pink facades with tiled roofs — climbs in terraces up the lower slopes; hundreds of tiny window lights already glowing gold in the blue shade. The town is small in scale, a distant band across the middle-left of the frame.

Background: layered mountain ridges of the Maritime Alps rise directly behind the town, left of center, with a prominent double-peaked rocky massif catching pink-orange sunlight on its upper faces while its lower slopes fall into cool blue-violet shadow. Fainter, hazier ridges recede to the far left and right horizon. On the far right the sea meets a low, flat horizon under the sun's afterglow, with a soft golden light path on the water.

No large animals dominate the scene; at most a few tiny distant seabirds as specks over the bay, correctly minuscule against the cliffs and buildings.

Lighting and atmosphere: late golden hour just after sunset, warm low light raking in from the right where the sky burns pale gold and peach near the horizon; long soft shadows, warm rim light on the palace stone and the cliff edges, cool blue shade filling the foreground hillside and the bay. Sky fills the upper half: deep blue at the top with dramatic wind-swept cirrus and altocumulus streaks lit hot pink, coral and magenta, cooling to violet-grey in the upper left corner. Color palette of coral pink, peach gold, deep cobalt and indigo blue, warm cream stone, olive and pine green.
