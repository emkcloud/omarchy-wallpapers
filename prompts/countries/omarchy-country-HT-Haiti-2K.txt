A cinematic 16:9 photorealistic digital painting of a golden-hour landscape in northern Haiti, on the Caribbean coast of Hispaniola, seen from a high mountain road that winds down toward the sea. The scene is built in three clear depth layers.

FOREGROUND (lower third and left edge): a wide, freshly paved two-lane asphalt road enters at the bottom center-right and snakes back into the picture in a long S-curve, first bending left across the lower-center, then doubling right and climbing along the ridge toward the middle distance. The road occupies roughly the lower quarter of the frame at its nearest point, about six to seven meters wide, with a warm yellow center line and a thin white edge line, subtle tar patches, and grit catching the low sun. Its left shoulder is cut into a rusty-ochre earth embankment topped by dense tropical undergrowth: broad banana and heliconia leaves, wild grasses, small pink and white flowering shrubs. On the far left, occupying the whole left edge from top to bottom, stands a large mature flamboyant (royal poinciana, Delonix regia) with a pale grey twisting trunk and a flat spreading canopy of vivid scarlet-orange blossoms that overhangs the top-left corner of the frame; the tree is about ten meters tall, correctly dwarfing nothing but the roadside bushes. A tiny Hispaniolan trogon perches on one of its lower branches, no larger than a hand — realistically small, almost incidental. On the right shoulder of the road, midway back, a small rusted metal road sign on a thin post, and beyond it a modest tin-roofed rural house with weathered blue-grey corrugated metal, only about as tall as the surrounding palms, half hidden by banana plants.

MIDGROUND (center and right): steep emerald hillsides of tropical vegetation, scattered coconut and royal palms of realistic height rising above the canopy. On the right and center-right, a small Caribbean coastal town spreads down the slope to the shore — dozens of tiny low houses with brightly painted walls in coral, turquoise, yellow and white, with silver corrugated tin roofs catching the sunlight, packed along narrow lanes among palms. Below them a pale sandy crescent beach with gentle turquoise surf.

BACKGROUND: dominating the upper center of the frame, a colossal stone mountaintop fortress in the manner of the Citadelle Laferrière: a massive early-19th-century citadel of grey-tan cut stone crowning a conical green peak, with high battered curtain walls, a sharp prow-like bastion pointing left, several rounded and squared corner towers, tiny cannon embrasures and rows of small windows. It reads as immense — many stories tall, larger than any building in the town — yet correctly scaled to its mountain, sitting like a crown on the summit with sheer cliffs and green forest falling away beneath it. Behind and left of it, layered blue-hazy mountain ridges recede toward the horizon. On the right, the coastline curves away into distant headlands and a wide, calm Caribbean sea that meets a bright horizon.

LIGHT AND COLOR: late-afternoon golden hour, the sun low on the right just above the sea, casting warm amber light across the fortress walls, the town's tin roofs and the road surface, with long soft shadows falling to the left. Sky graded from warm gold and peach near the horizon on the right to clear cerulean blue at the upper left, with soft cumulus clouds lit orange-pink underneath. Palette: emerald and olive greens, scarlet-orange blossoms, warm stone grey-tan, turquoise and aquamarine sea, golden amber highlights, cool blue mountain haze. Atmosphere: humid tropical air, gentle light haze in the valleys, peaceful and monumental.

STYLE: photorealistic cinematic digital painting, matte-painting quality, wide-angle vista, deep depth of field, natural realistic proportions, highly detailed, crisp textures, 16:9 wallpaper composition.
